Accuracy by spot type

    Settings

    Modes: Preflop drills opening, facing raises, 3-bets, 4-bets, squeezes and limpers. Postflop drills c-bets, defending, floating, barrels, river spots, check-raises and multiway pots. Exploit gives every spot a player read and grades the exploitative line. Mixed shuffles all of it. Missed spots are saved so you can replay them from the stats card until you get them right.

    Cash-game assumptions: 2.5bb opens (3bb from the small blind), 3-bets of 3x in position and 4x out of position, no antes. New players are "unknown" until you have showdowns; a 40bb stack models a fresh rebuy. With a straddle on, opens are 5bb, the straddle defends like a big blind, the big blind plays like a small blind, and everyone is effectively half as deep. Opponent ranges and the equity check are approximations built from the same charts, adjusted for the read you set.
